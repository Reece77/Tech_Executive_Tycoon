# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are recorded here. Golden snapshot regenerations must be listed with their reason.

## Unreleased

- M7a.0 turn speed: a save's SQLAlchemy engine (and its compiled-SQL cache) is kept between sessions instead of built per session, so statements are no longer recompiled every turn; the ledger balances are remembered with the state after a turn or action instead of summed over the whole ledger (golden seed 1's `TST_STATE_CACHE_CHECK=1` compares them too); a turn's row updates run as Core executemany statements (reveal records batched across people), and ledger entries and lines, morale log, product months, snapshots, log and inbox rows insert as one executemany per table; monthly growth reads skills once per person. The product-perf save (25 staff, 3 products, all rivals) averages 131 ms a persisted turn over 120 turns (was 266 ms; turns 110–120: 131 ms, was 215 ms), and the earn-and-burn script's pure-sim turn 45 ms (was 52). Golden snapshots unchanged.
- M6.0 CI and reads: CI runs as two matrix jobs, `check (shard 1/2)` (backend lint, types, test shard 1) and `check (shard 2/2)` (API sync, frontend, test shard 2), 25 minutes each (was one 45-minute job): 14m09s and 12m35s, against 26m40s for the one job at the start of M6; backend tests split by measured duration (`--shard K/N`, `tests/durations.json`, `make test-durations`). Read routes share one state load (`read_session`): a screen's seven parallel reads took about 6 s each and failed five e2e flows; now 0.35 s cold, 0.03 s warm. Golden snapshots unchanged.
- M6.9 balance, replay and speed: the director's quiet-month fill (Reece, 2026-10-29: when every triggered candidate is in cooldown, quiet-month templates fill the remaining slots at minor severity) and tuned `events.*` keys (count weights [0.08, 0.40, 0.38, 0.14], template cooldown 12, person cooldown 4, `underpaid` 0.95, `star_performer` top 30%, `team_clash` −25, `team_bond` 30, `board_unhappy` 50, minor morale cap 3; formulas.md §16.1, §12). §16.10 on 10 seeds × 120 turns (`test_event_balance.py`): 1.23 events a month, no critical event, event cash −1.44% of revenue (worst −2.24%), morale +1.32 (worst seed +3.22), 41 distinct templates; the M5 targets still hold. Replay reproduces a save with events, choices and a storyline without calling any adapter (`test_event_replay.py`). Speed (sub-task 0): new saves copy a once-migrated schema instead of running every migration; a save known to be at head is not re-checked; a turn updates only its own save's index entry; leftover unwritten events are found inside the turn's transaction; a process remembers the state it last wrote to a save and reuses it while the file is unchanged (`live_view` mirrors `load_state`; golden seed 1 runs with `TST_STATE_CACHE_CHECK=1`, which compares every reuse with a fresh load); tests share one `fresh_game` per arguments. Golden snapshots regenerated for this reason: with `MockLLMAdapter` events now apply their first choice at Continue (cash, morale and the people who stay move from the first months), and the script answers critical events with their first choice.
- M6.8 screens: events in the inbox with an Event (gold) or Critical (orange) badge open as a card (category, severity, storyline step, title, body, two or three full-width choices with their effects in words and sizes, never numbers or caps); a header line counts the events to answer and links the month's AI spend to Settings; "Ongoing stories" lists active storylines; a critical event opens a modal that cannot be dismissed and Continue waits ("Answer the critical event first"); Settings (top-bar button): Claude or offline templates, whether a key was found, the month's spend against the budget with calls and reset date, the budget, the two model ids, three counts and what Claude can and cannot see; the profile's Relationships panel (labels only); the org unit panel's Team mood line; AI headlines on Market news; minutes in the board's last meeting; exit-interview quotes on resignations.
