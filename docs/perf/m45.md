## Where the time went (local, interleaved A/B, CPU a month)

`tools/bench.py` builds the three saves: `v6`, `v6i`, and `dynasty` (708 months, `--length dynasty --children 2,5`). The per-phase split is from `tools/m30_profile.py` and labelled timers. The containers here run at x1.06-1.25 of the reference, so the comparison is relative: base (`40eefe5`, before any engine change) against the branch, on copies of one save, interleaved.

| Save | Base | After `83f9c60` | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| V6 `research_heavy` | 583 ms | 524 ms | -10% |
| V6 `full_integrator` | 660 ms | 615 ms | -7% |
| Dynasty 2069 | 803 ms | 674 ms | -16% |

`advance_turn` alone on the `full_integrator` save fell 388 to 336 ms (-13%) at `597c216`, and further with the later commits. The work, by sub-task:

- **Rival decisions (2):** the state's product, segment, region and regional-market lookups read indexes built once per tuple (`from_inputs`), in the tuple's order. `region_unserved` and `_rivals_in` read per-state indexes of live, priced products by segment. Remembered shares are written in one `with_segment_shares`. Kept values are checked against a recompute by `tests/unit/test_m30_memo.py` (`MEMO_CHECK`).
- **Per-turn saving (3):** reveal and skill changes are written by the raw driver's `executemany`, with the same SQL SQLAlchemy would send. A person's skills are diffed only when the tuple changed, and inserts go through `_insert_values`. Month-end MRR and operating histories are one `with_companies`. The turn's queries were already indexed (decision 6).
- **Market and history (4):** per-company ledger lines are indexed once a month (`_company_lines`) for the snapshot, operating costs and debt history. Era adoption factors, scale-up modifiers, monthly costs, rankings, the regulators' open and closed cases, groups and founders are each kept with their inputs. The living world's long-run costs are computed once per good and region a month.
- **Save size (5):** `tst.db.history.prune_candidates` (decision 4).
