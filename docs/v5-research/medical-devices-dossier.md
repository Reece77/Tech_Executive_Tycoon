## US routes (FDA)

- **510(k):** FDA's review goal is 90 FDA days; total time to decision is typically 4–6 months, longer with additional-information requests *(verify: FDA MDUFA performance reports)*. About 3,000 clearances a year *(verify)*.
- **De Novo:** FDA's goal is 150 review days; totals are often 8–12 months or more *(verify)*. Created 1997 and reformed in 2012 (FDASIA) *(verify)*.
- **PMA:** statutory 180 days, but in practice often a year or more of review, after pivotal studies that take years *(verify)*. About 30–40 original PMAs a year *(verify)*.
- **AI-enabled devices:** the FDA's public list passed about 1,000 authorisations by 2024, mostly radiology and mostly by 510(k) *(verify)*. Guidance on predetermined change control plans was finalised in December 2024 *(verify)*.
- **Apple Watch ECG:** De Novo in September 2018 *(verify)*.

## EU

- The **MDR** (Regulation (EU) 2017/745) has applied since **26 May 2021**. Legacy transition was extended in 2023 to 2027 or 2028 by class *(verify)*. Notified-body capacity and certification backlogs are shown in Commission surveys *(verify)*.
