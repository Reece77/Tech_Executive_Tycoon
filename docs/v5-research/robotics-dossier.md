China about half of installations from the early 2020s *(verify each year and region)*.
- **Operational stock:** about 4.3 million (2023) *(verify)*.
- **Robot density:** robots per 10,000 manufacturing workers by country (Korea, Singapore, China, Germany, Japan, US) *(verify)*.
- **Prices:** typical six-axis industrial robot about $50,000–100,000 including integration; collaborative robots about $25,000–50,000 *(verify)*.
- **Makers' shares:** FANUC, ABB, Yaskawa, KUKA (Midea from 2017), Kawasaki, Epson, Universal Robots (Teradyne from 2015); Chinese makers' domestic share rising past 40% by 2023 *(verify)*.

## Robotaxis

- **Waymo:** driverless rides in Phoenix (2020), San Francisco (2023–2024), Los Angeles (2024), Austin and Atlanta (2025, with Uber); paid rides about 250,000 a week by 2025 *(verify)*.
- **Cruise:** permit suspended by California in October 2023 after a pedestrian incident; GM ended the robotaxi programme in December 2024 *(verify)*.
- **Baidu Apollo Go:** driverless in Wuhan and other Chinese cities; rides a quarter by 2024–2025 *(verify)*. **Pony.ai, WeRide** (listed 2024) *(verify)*.
- **Tesla:** robotaxi service launched in Austin in June 2025 with safety monitors *(verify)*. **Zoox:** Las Vegas public service in 2025 *(verify)*.
- **Unit economics:** cost per mile estimates and vehicle cost for robotaxi fleets *(verify)*.
