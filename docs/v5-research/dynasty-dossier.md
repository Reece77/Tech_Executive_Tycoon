# v5 research dossier: dynasty mode (for M37)

**Status:** starting notes (2026-11-16). Dynasty mode is mostly seeded, but its rules should be anchored in published long-run trends.

- **Process scaling:** IRDS (International Roadmap for Devices and Systems) roadmaps to the late 2030s; announced TSMC, Intel and Samsung nodes; analyses of slowing cost per transistor (for example, cost per transistor flat since about 28 nm by some measures) *(verify)*.
- **Candidate eras for 2040–2065:** fusion power (ITER's schedule, private fusion roadmaps); general-purpose humanoids; brain–computer interfaces (first human implants 2024); fault-tolerant quantum computing (vendors' roadmaps); space industry (launch cost per kg trends); molecular and atomic-scale manufacturing. Each with a window and a domain, kept plausible rather than predicted.
- **Mortality:** life tables for high-income men and women aged 75–100 (for example US SSA or ONS period life tables), scaled down for wealth (life-expectancy gaps by income, Chetty et al. 2016) *(verify)*.
- **Leadership tenure:** median CEO tenure in large companies (about 7–10 years) and founder-CEO retirement ages *(verify)*.
- **Long-run growth:** mature-industry growth rates for categories that stop being new (for example PCs after 2011, smartphones after 2016) *(verify)*.
