## Sales and shares

- Global electric car sales (battery-electric and plug-in hybrid): about 17 million in 2024 (over 20% of new cars), and over 20 million expected in 2025 (more than one in four), per the IEA's [Global EV Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2025/executive-summary) and [press release](https://www.iea.org/news/more-than-one-in-four-cars-sold-worldwide-this-year-is-set-to-be-electric-as-ev-sales-continue-to-grow) *(verify yearly series 2010–2025 and regional shares: China about half of new car sales electric in 2024, Europe about 20%, US about 10%)*.
- Earlier years from the IEA's yearly outlooks (2010: tens of thousands; 2015: about 0.5 million; 2020: about 3 million; 2021: about 6.6 million; 2022: about 10 million; 2023: about 14 million) *(verify)*.

## Policy

- **China:** NEV purchase subsidies 2009–2022; purchase-tax exemption extended to 2027 with a taper *(verify)*.
- **US:** federal credit up to $7,500; the Inflation Reduction Act's sourcing rules from 2023; credit ended 30 September 2025 *(verify)*.
- **EU:** CO2 fleet standards (2020/2021 targets, 2025 step, 2035 zero-emission rule) *(verify)*.
- **Tariffs:** the US at 100% on Chinese EVs from 2024 (Section 301); the EU's countervailing duties on Chinese battery-electric cars from October 2024 (up to 35.3% on top of 10%) *(verify)*.
