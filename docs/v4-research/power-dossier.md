## Plant costs and build times by type (2010–2025)

Sources to use: Lazard's annual Levelized Cost of Energy+ (2010–2025), the US EIA's Capital Cost and Performance Characteristics reports (2013, 2016, 2020, 2024), Berkeley Lab's Utility-Scale Solar and Land-Based Wind reports, and IEA/NEA Projected Costs of Generating Electricity (2010, 2015, 2020).

## Interconnection queues

- Berkeley Lab's *Queued Up* series: capacity seeking US interconnection and median time from request to operation. [2024 edition](https://emp.lbl.gov/publications/queued-2024-edition-characteristics) (end of 2023: about 2,600 GW queued; median wait about 5 years for projects built in 2023, up from about 3 years in 2015) *(verify)*; [2025 edition](https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/publications/queued-2025-edition-characteristics).
