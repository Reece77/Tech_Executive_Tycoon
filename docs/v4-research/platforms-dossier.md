## Mobile platform shares (installed base or usage, by region and year)

- **Global smartphone usage shares, approximate** *(verify each against StatCounter's yearly series, [gs.statcounter.com](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile-/worldwide/2010))*:
  - 2010: Symbian about 35–40%, Android about 20–25%, iOS about 15–25%, BlackBerry about 15%, Windows under 5%;
  - 2013: Android about 60%, iOS about 25%;
  - 2016: Android about 70%, iOS about 20%;
  - 2020–2025: Android about 70–73%, iOS about 27–29%.
- **Regional shape** *(verify)*: iOS about 55–60% in the US from the late 2010s; about 30% in Europe; about 20–25% in China, where Android runs Chinese stores (Google Play unavailable).
- **End dates** *(verify)*: Symbian (Nokia's move to Windows Phone, 2011; last devices 2013), Windows Phone (support ended 2017; Windows 10 Mobile 2019–2020), BlackBerry 10 (devices ended 2016; services 2022).
- Statista's series ([global mobile OS share since 2009](https://statista.com/statistics/272670/global-market-share-held-by-mobile-operating-systems-since-2009)) can cross-check.

## Developers and catalogues

- App counts *(verify)*: App Store about 2.2M at its 2017 peak, about 1.8–2M since; Google Play about 3.5M at its 2017–2018 peak, about 2–2.5M after clean-ups.
- Registered developers *(verify)*: Apple has cited about 34M (2022); Google about 2–3M active publishers on Play.
- Global developer population *(verify)*: about 20M (2015) to about 27–30M (2025) professional developers (industry estimates).

## Standards

- USB-C over Lightning: the EU common-charger rule for phones from 28 December 2024; iPhone 15 moved to USB-C in September 2023 *(verify)*.
- Qi over PMA for wireless charging: PMA merged into AirFuel, 2015; Qi dominant from about 2017 *(verify)*.
