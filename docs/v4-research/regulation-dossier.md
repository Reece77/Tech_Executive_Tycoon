## Designations and duties

- EU Digital Markets Act: in force November 2022; gatekeepers designated September 2023 (Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, ByteDance, Meta, Microsoft; Booking in May 2024); obligations from 7 March 2024. Thresholds: 45M monthly end users and 10,000 business users in the EU, and €7.5B EU turnover or €75B market value. [European Commission](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/designated-gatekeepers-must-now-comply-all-obligations-under-digital-markets-act-2024-03-07_en) *(verify thresholds)*.
- Japan: Mobile Software Competition Act, full effect December 2025 *(verify)*. Korea: app payment law, 2021 *(verify)*. UK: Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, strategic market status designations from 2025 (Google search, Apple and Google mobile) *(verify)*.

## Major cases, fines and remedies (2010–2025)

- EU v Google: Shopping (2017, €2.42B, upheld 2024), Android (2018, €4.34B, reduced to €4.125B in 2022), AdSense (2019, €1.49B, annulled 2024) *(verify)*.
- EU v Apple: music streaming anti-steering (March 2024, about €1.8B); DMA non-compliance (April 2025, €500M) *(verify)*; EU v Meta: DMA "pay or consent" (April 2025, €200M) *(verify)*.
- US v Google (search): liability ruling August 2024; remedies decision September 2025 (no Chrome divestiture; data-sharing and exclusivity limits) *(verify)*. US v Google (ad tech): liability ruling April 2025 *(verify)*.
- Epic v Apple: 2021 injunction; April 2025 contempt ruling *(verify)*. Epic v Google: jury verdict December 2023; remedies 2024, upheld 2025 *(verify)*.
- FTC v Meta (Instagram and WhatsApp): filed 2020, trial 2025 *(verify outcome)*.
- Blocked or abandoned deals: Nvidia–Arm (2022), Adobe–Figma (2023) *(verify)*; Microsoft–Activision cleared with remedies (2023).
