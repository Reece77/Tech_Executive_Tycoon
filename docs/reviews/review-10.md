# Review 10 — `m59-done`

Reviewed `main` at `f38146d7` against `m45-done`: M46–M55, M58 and M59 (M56 and M57 are not merged). Read-only source review against `CLAUDE.md`, the ADRs, review 9 and each milestone's brief. Nothing was pushed, merged or committed; this file is the only change.

**How it was done.** Seven passes, one per priority in the request, each reading the code paths end to end. Candidates were checked with small scripts against the pure functions and with headless runs in scratch folders. The "Status" column says how far each finding was taken:

- **Reproduced**: a script or run showed the failure.
- **Read**: confirmed by reading the path; not run.
- **Plausible**: the code allows it, but one step was not traced.

**Not done.** The full test suite, the nightly and weekly suites and the Playwright tests were not run. Nothing was run on Windows, so cross-platform float parity and the installer are source checks only. `v6.0.0` is the merge after M48, so the old-save check covers saves at `0065_m47` crossing `0066`–`0070`; those saves were early-game (no revenue), and no late-game v6.0.0 save was produced.

## Checked and found sound

**Determinism**
- Stream registry: 70 names, no duplicate ids; ids 70–76 from the three lanes are distinct and match ADR-0013. A 2010 game's spawn keys are unchanged by the negative-turn mapping.
- No clock, filesystem, environment, `random`, module-level `np.random`, `hash()` or `uuid` in added `tst/sim` code. New float maths goes through `dmath`. Sets are used for membership or sorted first (finding 15 is the exception). Every `id()`-keyed cache holds its inputs; review 9's finding 9 and 12 fixes are present.
- Conversations draw only at opening, from seed and state; no draw depends on LLM text or round count. Entity keys for bank runs, stress tests, catastrophes, FX, title audiences and auctions are stable ids.
- Runs: seed 42 Sandbox from 2025 to August 2028 gave identical tables under `PYTHONHASHSEED` 1 and 777, and one warm process matched 43 cold single-turn processes. The player owned no telecom, bank, insurer or media assets in those runs.

**Money**
- Every new ledger entry in fx, finserv, media and telecom balances by construction; a 48-month bank run asserted zero-sum on every entry and reconciled account 1060 to the bank's reserves.
- Translation and forward valuation are exact-integer half-even, and settlement uses the same month's spot as translation. Content and spectrum amortisation use cumulative targets, so they do not drift. Bank interest, charge-offs and dividend caps are integer.
- The money guard scans every new package and has no new skip entries. Negotiations hand agreed integers to existing actions.

**Hidden values**
- Negotiation, meeting and talk APIs and prompts read only through `public()`; effects and votes appear only when a conversation is done.
- Stress shocks, bank runs and catastrophes are drawn in their month and only past ones are served. FX stores no future index. Rival auction values are never stored; seeded auctions, bans, eras and strikes are filtered by announcement month (they are pre-drawn into state, but no view, prompt or editor path serialises them). Title audience and quality are served only after release; ego and loyalty never reach the API.
- The editor, preview and custom scenarios read no save; the preview builds turn 0 only. Desktop routes expose no game state.

**LLM safety**
- Player text is stripped of control characters, capped at 600 characters and embedded only through `json.dumps` in the user turn, stored history included. Scenario labels reach no prompt.
- Classification is checked against the closed move set per kind; anything else is `neutral`. The engines enforce one move of each kind, at most three, with per-round clamps. Template buttons use the same engine functions. Replays never call the adapter. Skip draws nothing.
- The budget is one counter for all three kinds and all call types, per calendar month, shared by every save in the folder, read server-side and checked before each call (overshoot at most one call). A cap of 0 gives template mode.

**Interactions**
- M43a's and pharma's ceilings are in reference dollars and never read translated prices. Nothing draws FX before 2026 and there is no jump at the switch. Banks abroad are untranslated on both sides (ADR-0025 point 1).
- Finance, telecom, media and FX data cover 2008–2009; a 2008 campaign ran 40 months and a 2009 Sandbox 30 without error. No `turn >= 0` or turn-indexed list assumptions in the new packages.
- M34's share tests, fines and orders apply to media, finance and telecom lines. Custom starts in 2017, 2023 and 2025 with all 75 new incumbents removed played through the dated events without error.
- The custom scenario's canonical text has sorted keys, typed numbers and LF endings; NaN and infinity are refused. Achievements skip sandbox, custom and endless games.
