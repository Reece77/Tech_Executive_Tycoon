# v5 outline: The dynasty and new industries

**Status: OUTLINE (2026-11-14).** Scope, decisions and rough milestones. Full briefs, formulas and research come milestone by milestone. Historical figures are placeholders for the v5 research dossiers (`docs/v5-research/`).

## What v5 is for

By 2039 the player has built a conglomerate. v5 makes the game about **the family and what comes after**, and puts **new industries** on the chain that v3 and v4 built: cars, robots, space, medical devices and medicines.

M24 and M26 already built much of the founder's life: stress and burnout, the persona, political influence and scandals, the foundation, personal investing and the family office. v5 builds on them.

## Reece's decisions (2026-11-14, "defaults", plus pharmaceuticals)

1. **Succession:** the player **plays the heir**, a new character who takes over the company. The old founder becomes a board member and adviser with opinions.
2. **Past 2039:** an optional **dynasty mode** continues to **2069**, with seeded eras beyond history.
3. **New industries:** **electric vehicles and robotics first**, then space, medical devices and **pharmaceuticals** (Reece's addition).
4. **Medical devices** with **realistic approval stages** (FDA-style 510(k) and PMA, trials, recalls). **Pharmaceuticals** are equally realistic: trial phases with historical odds, patents and the generics cliff, and pricing by region.

## Data needs (dossiers, before each brief)

- **M36:** estate and inheritance tax by region (rates, exemptions and dates); typical education paths and costs; succession history for founder-led companies.
- **M38:** EV sales and shares by region, 2010–2025; battery pack cost per kWh by year; charging network sizes; rival line-ups.
- **M41:** FDA 510(k), De Novo and PMA timelines and approval rates; EU MDR (2021); recall rates; device market sizes.
- **M42:** phase-transition success rates by therapeutic area (published industry studies); cost per approved drug; trial durations; patent terms and exclusivity periods; generic price erosion after expiry; pricing and reimbursement by region; landmark AI-discovered molecules.
