# M23c: Playtest 5 fixes and the interface (lane C)

**Goal:** fix what Reece's v3 playtest found hard to use, and make the shell feel like Football Manager: bars that stay put, critical decisions you can research before making, and screens that say how to proceed rather than only what's missing. Make the practice saves realistic.

**Playtest:** `docs/playtests/reece-playtest-5.md` · **Formulas:** §18, §31–§37 (display only) · **Depends on:** M22 (`m22-done`) · **Lane:** C, alongside M23a and M23b

## Lane

- Its own session and branch (`m23c-interface`). Merges whenever ready, before M23b.
- **M23c owns:** the front end (`frontend/src/`), API view builders and read-only endpoints for display, the practice-save tools (`tools/e2e_*_save.py` and the helpers they call in `tst/balance/` for fixtures only), `fix-fixture-reveals`'s change (already merged into this docs branch), glossary entries.
- **M23c must not edit** simulation rules (`backend/tst/sim/`) except read-only helpers for display; anything that needs a rule change goes to M23a as a note. M23b's strategies and targets are off limits.

## Sub-tasks (commit after each)

1. **The shell:** the top bar (date, cash, energy, stress, Continue) and the side navigation stay visible while the working pane scrolls independently, at every screen width.
2. **Look before you decide:** a critical event can be **set aside** to browse any screen read-only (Continue stays blocked until it's answered), with a persistent banner linking back to it; the event card links to the screens that matter for it (a mine's panel for a mine crash, the product for a recall).
3. **Exploration:** clicking a block opens a **licence confirmation** (cost, term, what you get) instead of buying at once; every greyed-out stage says **how** to unlock it ("drill 18 more holes (40 in total), then re-estimate"); a **progress line** toward the next resource category; a **"Your blocks and projects"** view across all regions; desk-study results kept per region and commodity, so running one doesn't recolour the map you were looking at.
4. **The group:** **Found a subsidiary** on the Group screen (Finance → Group), linked from Mines; pitches and capital requests link to the screen where they're answered; a **capital-actions history** (stake sales, carve-outs, spin-offs, buyouts: date, proceeds, ownership before and after, NCI, value created); subsidiaries' **quarterly updates** in their CEO's voice, each ending with a question the player can answer (advice that sets a nudge on the mandate).
5. **Chips, wafers and foundries (wording):** "wafer contract" becomes **foundry contract**; the wafer plant makes **blank wafers**; a chip's cost shows **wafer material** and **foundry processing** separately; a chip used inside the group's devices shows "**used in** N phones" instead of no revenue; "used" figures on own-supply lines get units and a tooltip.
6. **Commodities planning:** a planning view per commodity: need over the next 12 months from current sales and plans, which products drive it, and how much offtakes and own supply cover.
7. **Explain and glossary:** NPV, IRR, reserves, grade and yield in the glossary; Explain on a mine's NPV and cost per unit, a fab's cost per good die, and the v3 screens (Infrastructure, Hardware, Research, Group, Mines, Refineries, Fabs, World), as M25's follow-up asked.
8. **Angel stakes:** show the last-round value and an **estimated value range** from the startup's public growth since its round, labelled as an estimate, beside the last-round mark.
9. **Realistic practice saves:** stage-3 and larger fixtures get a believable team (a CTO, product and sales staff, the specialists the scenario needs); a **chip company with its own fab** fixture; `mature` stops claiming chips it doesn't have; every fixture's People and Org screens open (a fixture test opens People, Org and the fixture's main screen for each mode).
10. **e2e and close-out:** Playwright flows for the sticky shell, setting aside a critical event, the licence confirmation and the planning view; CHANGELOG; README index row.

## Decisions made

No simulation rule changed; `backend/tst/sim/` is untouched. Golden snapshots unchanged (run on the branch).

1. **The shell.** `.tst-shell` fills the window (`100dvh`); only the side nav and the working pane (`data-testid="working-pane"`) scroll. Under 800 px the nav becomes a strip (at most 30% of the height) under the top bar, which stays put too.
2. **Setting a critical event aside** is front-end state (sessionStorage per save, keyed by the blocking event's id), so it ends by itself once the event is answered. "Read only" is enforced in the client: while set aside, an `openapi-fetch` middleware (`src/lib/setAside.ts`) answers every non-GET request with a 409 `read_only` message, except answering the event and the requests that change nothing (previews, quotes, hints, inbox marks, Explain and the chief of staff's questions, table views). Continue stays blocked by the engine as before.
3. **Event links** (`EventOut.links`, `tst/api/event_links.py`): the event's product, people, rival and loan, the mine, refinery or project whose open event it is (found as the engine's answer finds it), the category's screen, and Cash and statements. Mines, Refineries and Exploration open the linked item from `?mine=`, `?plant=`, `?project=`; the inbox links group, finance and exploration items too (a subsidiary's capital request: "Answer it on the Group screen").
4. **How to unlock** is a `how` beside every `reason` (exploration stages, renewals, mine quotes, the founding gate, refinery quotes, fab quotes and conversions), written in `tst/api/howto.py` from the same public rules the engine checks (hole counts by category: 8, 20, 40; a stage's due month; a node's first orderable month; the process level needed). The refinery quote gains `allowed`/`reason`/`how` for a by-product line without a host plant, which the Build button previously only refused on click. The scan test requires every disallowed item to carry a `how` that starts with a next step and differs from its reason.
5. **Licence confirmation** is a dialog in the front end (fee, a 5-year term renewable once, the block's bands, what the licence gives); the action is unchanged. Desk studies were already kept per region and commodity in the API; the map now lists every study as a chip that switches the map, and running a study never changes the map shown. "Your blocks and projects" is the projects table with a region and a next-step column, and picking a row switches the map to its region.
6. **Found a subsidiary** moved to Finance → Group (the same form and action); Mines links there. Capital requests sit first on the Group screen.
7. **Capital-actions history** (`GET /group/history`) reads the entries the engine posted for each stake sale, carve-out, spin-off and buyout (by memo) and the ownership rebases: proceeds are the entry's cash line, ownership before and after from the rebases (several actions in one month in posting order), NCI = the minorities' share of book equity after it, and **value created** = the entry's equity line (what a sale fetched over the book value given up); a buyout's is the minority's book value less the price (negative: a premium paid); a spin-off's is the investment distributed to shareholders.
8. **Quarterly updates** (`GET /group/updates`) are built on read from public numbers (the latest quarter's revenue against the one before, operating margin, cash, the mandate's last review) with a question chosen by those numbers; nothing is stored. The advice buttons re-set the mandate through the existing `set_mandate` action (3 energy), keeping its capex limit, categories and regions. A lighter "nudge" that is not a mandate change would be a rule: **note to M23a** below.
9. **Wording.** "Foundry contract" in every screen and the API's messages; the wafer plant's label is "Blank wafer plant (300 mm)" (`balance.yaml` label only). Route and field names (`wafer-contracts`, `wafer_*`) stay. A chip's cost splits into **wafer material** (the blank wafer's price, the group's transfer price on its own wafers, over the good dies), **foundry processing** (the rest of the wafer part) and packaging; the three add up to the unit cost the engine posts. `ChipOut.used_in` names the group's own devices that take the chip ("used in N of your Forge Phone last month (at cost: its revenue is the devices')"). Own-supply lines carry the good's unit, with a tooltip on "used".
10. **The planning view** (`GET /commodities/plan`) projects 12 months at this month's production, using the same use per unit as the engine's commodity cost, split by product; deals cover the months they run; own supply is the group's own-plant supply this month at that rate; the group's mines' output is shown beside it (sold at market unless supplied to the group). It is a run-rate projection, not a demand forecast. The month field is `required` because `need` is an ADR-0007 forbidden name.
11. **Explain** gains four metrics and scopes: `mine_npv` (project), `mine_cost` (mine), `fab_die_cost` (fab), `refinery_cost` (refinery), in `tst/advisor/v3.py`; their related events are their facility's category and the world's. The coverage scan now maps every number of Infrastructure, Hardware, Research, Group, Mines, Refineries, Fabs and World's main models to a metric or a reason. Glossary entries for NPV, IRR, reserves, grade, yield, NCI, the capital history, the CEO updates, wafer material, foundry processing and the planning columns.
12. **Angel stakes**: `estimate_low_cents`/`estimate_high_cents` = the stake at the startup's displayed value range (`valuation.value_range_cents`, the M&A and Group screens' public range) while it is open, labelled "(est.)" beside the last-round value.
13. **Practice saves.** `tst/balance/fixtures.py` (fixtures only) gives a save a knowledge record for every hand-built employee and, for the v3 tools run from the command line, a stage-3 team in its own units: 4 senior software engineers, a DevOps engineer, 2 product managers, a designer, 3 sales representatives, an account executive, a marketer, a finance manager and a recruiter, the scenario's specialists (M18 a data scientist; M19 two mining engineers and an operations manager; M20 two hardware engineers and an operations manager; M22 and `chipfab` three chip designers and two hardware engineers), and a CTO appointed through the engine's action. M20-M22's saves are raised to stage 3 from the command line, as M18's and M19's already were (a company running refineries and fabs is a scale-up; seats open at stage 3). The mode functions the integration tests call directly are unchanged, so those tests keep their exact staff. New `e2e_m21_save.py chipfab`: `mature` with a live mobile SoC made on its own 28 nm fab; `mature`'s description now says it has no chips of its own. The m21 tool's `hire_unit` (a balance script helper, M23b's) is not changed; `finish` adds the records instead.
14. **The fixture test** (`tests/integration/test_m23c_fixtures.py`) runs each tool's command line in process and opens People, Org, the main screen and the newest hire's profile for every save-making mode (edit modes such as `advance` and `ripen` need one of them first). It found a second broken save, `e2e_m12a_save.py cloud` ("no person 3"), fixed with `finish`. The 21 v3 modes run in CI's `check`; the 49 older modes (about 30 CPU-minutes) are marked `nightly`, so `make check` and the nightly workflow run them. Both passed locally.

### Notes to M23a (rule changes this lane did not make)

- A subsidiary's advice as a **nudge** (a quarter's lean toward growth, margin or cash without a mandate change or its energy) would need a rule in `tst/sim/group/subsidiaries.py`.
- The engine's own refusal still says "a wafer contract" (`tst/sim/actions/products.py` line 207, and `tst/cli.py`'s `sign-wafer-contract` help): reword to "a foundry contract" when that file is next touched.
- Server-side read only (the engine refusing actions while a critical event is open) if the client guard is not enough.
