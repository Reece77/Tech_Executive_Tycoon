## Decisions made

- **Sub-task 0 (turn speed).** Profiled turns 100–120 of the product-perf save: writes 70 ms, sim 54 ms (people updates 35), event writing 24, ledger sums 21. Changes: a save's SQLAlchemy engine is kept between sessions (`tst.db.session.shared_engine`, 16 most recent saves; NullPool still closes the file after each session), because a new engine per session threw away the compiled-SQL cache and recompiled every statement every turn; the ledger balances are remembered with the state after each turn and action (`current_balances`; `TST_STATE_CACHE_CHECK=1` compares them with a fresh sum), because summing the whole ledger grows with every month; row updates are Core executemany statements (reveal records batched across people) instead of the ORM's bulk update; ledger entries and lines, morale log, product months, snapshots, log and inbox rows are inserted with one executemany per table; growth reads skills once per person. RNG stream construction (14 µs, about 6 ms a turn) is left as is: a faster derivation would change every draw. **Result:** 120 persisted turns average 131 ms (266 before); turns 110–120 131 ms (215 before); the earn-and-burn pure-sim turn 45 ms (52). Local suite 7m12s with `-n auto` on 4 cores; the slowest tests are pure-sim statistics (judgment 146 s, event balance 96 s, offers 76 s, delegates 75 s), which sub-task 2 moves to `nightly`.
- **Sub-task 1 (targets).** Approved by Reece on 2026-10-30 with three changes: success also needs V_traction > V_floor at turn 120; founder value for success and wins is the mean of turns 109–120; steady's standard bankruptcy band is 5–25%.
- **Sub-task 2 (runner).** `tst/balance/` (outside the sim): `game.py` plays a company over the pure sim with events written by `MockLLMAdapter` and records founder value and traction each month; `strategies.py` holds the four scripts, which act only through actions (job posts, interviews, offers at 1.05× the ask, rounds, bridges), so energy, cash and gates apply; a refused action is skipped and counted. `report.py` plays runs in worker processes (`--jobs`) and turns them into one row per target. Script numbers (round targets, hiring order, the 1.05× offer) are in `strategies.py`, not `balance.yaml`: they describe the scripts, not the game. E1 and E2 stay in `test_event_balance.py` (now `nightly`; E2 asserted on every seed); the runner prints E3. T6 counts leavers other than a company closing; T7 uses the do-nothing runs. Nine statistical tests are marked `nightly` (judgment, offer acceptance gap, delegates, underpaid misfits, events, rivals 1–4, funding); PR CI runs `-m "not nightly"`, `make nightly` and `.github/workflows/nightly.yml` run them, and `make check` still runs everything. **First full run:** 17.5 worker-minutes, 4m43s wall clock with 4 jobs; T1, steady's T3, T4a (steady), T4b, T4c, T5 (easy, blitzscale), T6 tenure and early leavers, and incumbents (T7) already pass. To fix in sub-task 4: bootstrap-frugal goes bankrupt by month 16–23 on every seed; steady wins all 10 seeds; blitzscale closes its seed in month 1 (band 3–12) and has its founder removed on 5 seeds; steady goes bankrupt on 0/10 standard seeds and 9/10 hard ones; 8% of staff leave a year; 98% of rival startups survive five years; a solo founder sees 3 distinct templates in 24 months, all finance or quiet-month.
- **Sub-task 3 (templates).** 22 new templates (82 in all): 14 in a new `founder` category on a new trigger `early_days` (§16.2: the player has no live product; strength 0.4, `events.trigger.early_days`; names the product in development, if any), plus personnel 4, market 2 and operations 2 on existing triggers. Before, a solo founder could only draw market and finance templates and, with no product, almost every month was quiet, so templates alone could not reach E3: the trigger is the smallest change that lets early-phase templates fire, and the `founder` category is the "founder templates" that §16.1 already allowed a company with no staff. `early_days` holds instead of `quiet_month`. Founder templates use only `{founder}`, `{company}` and `{product}`, with energy, cash, and product quality or brand effects, and never name the founder as a person (the 4-month person cooldown would throttle them). The new staffed templates default to morale-neutral first choices. **Result:** E3 met at 24 turns (solo founder: 15 distinct templates a run, 18% finance or quiet-month, at most 2 repeats; bootstrap before launch 3%); E1/E2 on the earn-and-burn script: 1.38 events a month, cash −1.34% of revenue (worst −2.08%), morale +1.21 (worst seed 4 at +2.90, inside ±3), 59 distinct templates. Golden snapshots regenerated: early-days events move cash and energy from month 1–2 on every seed.
- **Tags (D5).** `.github/workflows/tag-milestone.yml` runs when `check` completes on `main`; on success it reads the head commit's `Milestone-Tag:` trailer and, if that `mX-done` tag doesn't exist, creates an annotated tag and pushes it with `GITHUB_TOKEN` (contents: write). CLAUDE.md §6 and `docs/tasks/README.md` say so.
