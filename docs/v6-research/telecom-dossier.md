# v6 research dossier: telecom (for M53)

**Status:** starting list (2026-11-24). The M53 session builds `backend/data/telecom.yaml` from this, citing a source for every value, at the game's scale (§54).

- **Subscribers:** mobile subscriptions and unique subscribers by region, 2010–2025 (GSMA Intelligence, ITU); fixed broadband and fibre subscriptions by region (OECD, ITU) *(verify)*.
- **Generations:** 4G launch dates by region (Verizon's LTE December 2010; others); 5G commercial launches (South Korea and the US, April 2019); 5G population coverage by 2025 *(verify)*.
- **Spectrum auctions:** the US C-band auction (Auction 107, 2020–2021, about $81B gross), the 600 MHz incentive auction (2017), the UK's 5G auctions (2018, 2021), Germany's 2019 auction, India's 2022 auction *(verify)*.
- **Equipment:** RAN market shares (Huawei, Ericsson, Nokia, ZTE, Samsung) by year; US restrictions on Huawei (the 2019 Entity List; the FCC's rip-and-replace programme); the UK's 2020 decision to remove Huawei from 5G by 2027; EU guidance *(verify)*.
- **Operators:** revenues, subscribers and capex of the largest operators by region (Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile US, Vodafone, Deutsche Telekom, Orange, Telefónica, China Mobile, NTT Docomo, Reliance Jio, América Móvil) *(verify)*.
- **Subsea cables:** major routes and owners (TeleGeography's Submarine Cable Map), hyperscalers' share of new capacity *(verify)*.
- **Regulation:** US net-neutrality rules (2015 adopted, 2017 repealed, 2024 re-adopted then struck down in January 2025), EU open-internet rules (2015), operator mergers (T-Mobile and Sprint 2020) *(verify)*.
